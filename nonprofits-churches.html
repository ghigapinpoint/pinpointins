Safeguarding Your Mission, Board, and Staff

Nonprofits, community advocacy groups, and local churches share unique operational responsibilities. You are focused on serving people, leading congregations, and supporting vulnerable populations in our community—our role is to help ensure your pastors, staff, board, and volunteers are properly cared for.
