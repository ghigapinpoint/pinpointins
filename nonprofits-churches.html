Predictable Budgets for Nonprofits

When an organization operates on fixed grants and annual budgets, unexpected renewal increases are hard to absorb. Reviewing all major California carriers ahead of renewal helps keep your benefit costs steady.

Flexible Monthly Allowances

Tax-Free Health Stipends (QSEHRA)

For churches and nonprofits that aren't ready for a traditional group plan, a tax-free monthly health stipend lets you set a fixed dollar allowance while staff choose the individual plan that fits their family.
