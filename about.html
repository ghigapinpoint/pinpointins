Leadership & Client Operations

Greg Higa, Principal Broker — Greg started his insurance career in 2002 as a customer service representative. After several successful years helping clients resolve complex coverage questions, he made the transition to Account Executive. In 2010, he was offered the opportunity to expand and joined the ownership team of Level One Insurance Services.

In 2013, Level One was purchased, and Greg founded Pinpoint Solution Insurance Services LLC (PSIS) with a dedicated focus on putting clients first and building lasting relationships across the South Bay.

Daniel Park, Operations & Client Relations — Daniel oversees client response protocols, coverage triage workflows, and digital administration at Pinpoint Solution Insurance Services, helping South Bay families, employers, and local ministries compare networks and benefits with clarity.

Our Service Commitment: We stay in touch throughout the year with periodic reviews and ongoing enrollment assistance, and we take pride in our ability to respond to client inquiries within 24 hours.
