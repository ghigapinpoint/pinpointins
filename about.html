A Local Relationship That Lasts

Building a strong working relationship also means you have a familiar point of contact in Torrance. As your life or organization changes from year to year, Greg and Daniel are here to answer questions, handle enrollment updates, and review your renewal options.

Greg Higa

Greg started his insurance career in 2002 as a customer service representative, helping families and employers sort through everyday coverage questions, doctor networks, and claims. After several years in client service, he moved into an Account Executive role, working directly with business owners to set up and manage their employee benefit plans.

In 2010, Greg was offered the opportunity to expand his leadership and joined the ownership team of Level One Insurance Services. When Level One was purchased in 2013, Greg founded Pinpoint Solution Insurance Services LLC with a simple goal: to focus on his clients and build strong, long-term working relationships.
