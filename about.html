Our Story & Service Promise

Local Roots. Two Decades of Guidance.

Building strong working relationships and supporting South Bay businesses, families, and ministries with responsive, personal service.

Leadership & Experience

Greg Higa started his insurance career in 2002 as a customer service representative. After several successful years helping clients resolve complex coverage questions, he made the transition to Account Executive. In 2010, he was offered the opportunity to expand and joined the ownership team of Level One Insurance Services.

In 2013, Level One was purchased, and Greg founded Pinpoint Solution Insurance Services (PSIS) with a dedicated focus on putting clients first and building lasting relationships across the South Bay.

Our Service Commitment: We stay in touch throughout the year with periodic reviews and ongoing enrollment assistance, and we take pride in our ability to respond to client inquiries within 24 hours.
