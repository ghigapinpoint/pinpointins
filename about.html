Why Independent Local Guidance Matters

When you shop directly with a single carrier, you only see their plans. When you use a national online call center or rigid PEO, you rarely speak to the same person twice—and they often don't know the difference between Torrance Memorial (TMIPA), Providence Little Company of Mary, or Optum South Bay.

As an independent brokerage based on Hawthorne Boulevard in Torrance, we work solely for our clients. We also collaborate closely with local CPAs and Property & Casualty (P&C) brokers who want a dedicated, non-competing group health specialist for their commercial clients.
