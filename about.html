Why Relationship-First Advisory Matters

When you go directly to a single insurance carrier, you only see their plans. When you use a private exchange, purchasing alliance, or rigid PEO, you rarely speak to the same person twice—and no one is there to understand how your company, family, or ministry is changing from year to year.

Located in Torrance, we take the time to know our clients and the individual needs of each situation. Building a strong working relationship allows us to understand changes in your life or organization early, resolve billing or claim problems quickly, and make sure your coverage continues to serve you well long after enrollment.

Our Service & Stewardship Commitments

24-Hour Response Standard We take pride in our ability to respond within 24 hours whenever you have a question, renewal concern, or claim issue.
Whole-Truth Recommendations If your existing plan is already the wisest choice for your doctors and budget, we will tell you plainly and advise you to stay right where you are.
Year-Round Reviews & Enrollment Support After you sign up, we are still here—helping onboard new hires, checking doctor networks, and conducting periodic coverage reviews.

Meet Our Team

Greg Higa

Greg started his insurance career in 2002 as a customer service representative, where he learned firsthand how much families and employers depend on a patient, reliable advocate when navigating coverage questions and claims. After several successful years in client service, he made the transition to Account Executive, working closely with businesses to design and manage their employee benefit programs.

In 2010, Greg was offered the opportunity to expand his leadership and joined the ownership team of Level One Insurance Services. When Level One was purchased in 2013, Greg founded Pinpoint Solution Insurance Services LLC (PSIS) with a clear purpose: to lead a practice focused entirely on serving clients faithfully and building long-term relationships of deep trust.
