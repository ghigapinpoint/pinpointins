Employee Benefits That Fit Your Budget and Your People

Practical group health, life, and disability coverage for South Bay small businesses and nonprofits—structured without the corporate runaround.

Taking Care of Your Team Without the Complexities

Finding the right health and insurance benefits for a small team shouldn't feel like a puzzle. We help local businesses and nonprofits sort through group medical, dental, vision, life, and disability options to find plans that actually match your operating budget.

We know you aren't a massive corporation with an HR department of fifty people. Whether you're offering benefits for the first time or trying to make sense of a steep renewal increase, we sit down with you, look at the numbers together, and figure out a sustainable path forward.

Group Medical, Dental & Vision

We guide you through carrier selections (including major California networks like Kaiser, Anthem, and Blue Shield) to help weigh HMO, PPO, and HSA-compatible structures based on your team's actual healthcare habits.

Income Protection & Life

Short-term disability, long-term disability, and group life insurance policies are often surprisingly affordable, offering invaluable peace of mind to employees and their families during unexpected medical crises.

Smart Renewal Strategies

Instead of absorbing shock-wave rate hikes every year, we evaluate contribution strategies, tier levels, and cost-sharing options well ahead of your renewal date to keep your organization financially sound.
