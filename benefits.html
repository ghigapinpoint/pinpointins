Understanding Health Plan & Network Types

Each plan and carrier offers a variety of doctor networks to choose from. We help you and your employees weigh provider network size, primary care referrals, and premium costs.

Direct Specialist Access

PPO (Preferred Provider Organization)

Members can see any doctor or specialist—including direct access to UCLA Health and Torrance Memorial specialists—without a primary care referral. Out-of-pocket costs are lower when staying in-network.

Coordinated Local Care

HMO (Health Maintenance Organization)

Members select a primary care doctor inside a local medical group (such as TMIPA, Optum South Bay, Providence, or Kaiser Permanente) who coordinates care and specialist referrals with predictable copays.

In-Network No-Referral

EPO (Exclusive Provider Organization)

Members can see any doctor or specialist inside the carrier's network without needing a referral, while keeping premiums lower by excluding out-of-network coverage.

Tax-Advantaged Savings

HDHP & HSA-Qualified Plans

High Deductible Health Plans paired with tax-advantaged Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), allowing employers and employees to set aside pre-tax dollars for medical expenses.
