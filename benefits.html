Understanding Health Plan & Network Types

Each plan and carrier offers a variety of doctor networks to choose from. We help you and your employees weigh provider network size, primary care referrals, and premium costs.

PPO (Preferred Provider Organization)

Members can see any doctor or specialist—including direct access to UCLA Health and Torrance Memorial specialists—without a primary care referral. Procedure costs will be higher if you see a doctor outside of your plan's network.

HMO (Health Maintenance Organization)

Members select a primary care doctor inside an IPA (such as TMIPA, Optum South Bay, Providence, or Kaiser Permanente) who manages overall care and provides referrals to specialists.

EPO (Exclusive Provider Organization)

Members can see any doctor or specialist inside the carrier's network without needing a referral, but there is no coverage for out-of-network providers.

HDHP & HSA-Qualified Plans

High Deductible Health Plans where costs for services are the member's responsibility until the deductible is met. Many of these plans are paired with tax-advantaged Health Savings Accounts (HSAs).
