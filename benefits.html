2026–2027 California Carrier Update

Transitioning From a Health Net Group Plan

Health Net is discontinuing its California commercial employer-group health plans (including Health Net tiers inside CaliforniaChoice), with affected groups transitioning to a new carrier by February 28, 2027. (Note: Health Net's Individual & Family, Covered California, Medi-Cal, and Medicare plans are not affected.)

If your business, church, or nonprofit currently has a Health Net group policy, there is no need to panic—and you don't have to wait until the last minute. Most replacement carriers (including Blue Shield, Anthem, Kaiser, and UnitedHealthcare) are offering streamlined transition paperwork that accepts your recent Health Net invoice in place of quarterly payroll tax filings. Greg and Daniel can verify your employees' South Bay doctors across the replacement plans and handle the paperwork from start to finish at $0 added cost.
