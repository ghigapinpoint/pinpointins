5 Common Pitfalls When Evaluating Health Plans

  1. Assuming "One Size Fits All": Offering a single rigid plan when pairing HMO, PPO, or HSA tiers can better serve diverse needs at a lower cost.
  2. Overlooking Local IPA & Hospital Tiers: Not checking whether Torrance Memorial (TMIPA), Providence Little Company of Mary, UCLA Health, or Optum South Bay participate in a specific carrier's sub-network.
  3. Auto-Renewing Without Benchmarking: Accepting annual rate increases without reviewing competing carriers or adjusting contribution structures.
  4. Missing Tax-Advantaged Vehicles: Overlooking Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), SHOP tax credits, or QSEHRA/ICHRA reimbursements.
  5. Paying Full COBRA Rates Unnecessarily: Staying on expensive corporate COBRA when an individual Covered CA or direct plan covers the exact same South Bay doctors for much less.
