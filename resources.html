How to Print or Get a New Medical or Dental ID Card

If you misplaced your insurance card, recently enrolled, or have a doctor's appointment before your plastic card arrives in the mail, you don't have to wait. Every major California carrier lets you view or print a temporary card immediately—or our Torrance office can pull your member ID and order a replacement for you.

  • Blue Shield & Anthem Blue Cross: Log in to your member portal or mobile app to download a printable PDF card, or ask our office to email your temporary copy.
  • Kaiser Permanente: Open the Kaiser Permanente app to display your digital medical record number (MRN) at check-in, or call member services to mail a new card.
  • UnitedHealthcare, Aetna & Health Net: Digital ID cards are available inside each carrier's mobile app as soon as your policy is active.
  • Delta Dental & VSP Vision: Most dentists and optometrists only need your Primary Subscriber Name, Birthday, and Group/Member ID—a physical card is rarely required at the front desk.

5 Common Pitfalls When Comparing Plans

  1. Offering Only One Plan Option: Offering a single plan when pairing HMO, PPO, or HSA-compatible options can better fit different budgets and doctor preferences.
  2. Overlooking Local Doctor Group Rules: Not checking whether a specific carrier tier actually includes your doctors at Torrance Memorial (TMIPA), Providence Little Company of Mary, UCLA Health, or Optum South Bay.
  3. Renewing Without Comparing Other Carriers: Accepting annual rate increases without checking whether another carrier offers a better rate for the same doctors.
  4. Missing Tax-Free Health Stipends & HSAs: Overlooking Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), small-business tax credits, or tax-free monthly health allowances (QSEHRA).
  5. Paying Full COBRA Rates Unnecessarily: Staying on expensive corporate COBRA when an individual Covered California or direct plan covers the exact same South Bay doctors for much less.
