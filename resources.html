Top 5 Mistakes to Avoid When Choosing Your Health Plans

Selecting group or individual health coverage doesn't have to be overwhelming. Here are the five most common pitfalls we help South Bay employers and families avoid:

  1. Assuming "One Size Fits All": Offering a single rigid plan when a combination of HMO, PPO, or HSA tiers could better serve diverse employee needs at a lower cost.
  2. Overlooking Doctor & Hospital Networks: Failing to verify whether preferred local South Bay physicians and medical groups participate in a specific carrier's network tier.
  3. Auto-Renewing Without a Market Review: Accepting annual rate increases without comparing competing carriers or adjusting contribution structures.
  4. Missing Tax-Advantaged Vehicles: Overlooking employer tax deductions, Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), or Health Reimbursement Arrangements (HRAs).
  5. Going Without Ongoing Enrollment Support: Choosing a plan without a dedicated broker who stays in touch throughout the year to resolve claims, billing, or onboarding questions.
