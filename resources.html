5 Common Pitfalls When Evaluating Health Plans

  1. Assuming "One Size Fits All": Offering a single rigid plan when pairing HMO, PPO, or HSA-compatible tiers can better serve diverse family needs at a lower cost.
  2. Overlooking Local Doctor Group Rules: Not checking whether a specific carrier tier actually includes your doctors at Torrance Memorial (TMIPA), Providence Little Company of Mary, UCLA Health, or Optum South Bay.
  3. Auto-Renewing Without Benchmarking: Accepting annual rate increases without reviewing competing carriers or adjusting employer contribution structures.
  4. Missing Tax-Free Health Stipends & HSAs: Overlooking Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), small-business tax credits, or tax-free monthly health allowances (QSEHRA).
  5. Paying Full COBRA Rates Unnecessarily: Staying on expensive corporate COBRA when an individual Covered California or direct plan covers the exact same South Bay doctors for much less.
