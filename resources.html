Quick Member Guide

How to Print or Get a New Insurance ID Card

If you misplaced your card, recently enrolled, or have a doctor's appointment before your plastic card arrives in the mail, you can view or print a copy in just a few minutes:

  1. Sign In to Your Member Portal or App: Visit your carrier's member login page (Blue Shield, Anthem, Kaiser, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, or Health Net) or open their mobile app. If it is your first time signing in, you can register using your name, date of birth, and Social Security number or member ID.
  2. Click "ID Cards" or "Get ID Card": Once signed in, look for the ID Cards link in the main menu. From there, you can view your digital card on your phone, download a PDF to print at home, or click Order Replacement Card to have a new plastic card mailed to you.
  3. Visiting the Dentist or Optometrist (Delta Dental & VSP): Dental and vision plans rarely require a physical card at check-in. Simply give the front desk the primary subscriber's full name and date of birth, and they can pull up your eligibility right away.

Helpful Tips for Your Visit

Need Your Member ID Number Before Portal Registration? If a new policy just started and you need your Member ID number to set up your online account—or if you run into any trouble logging in—call our Torrance office at (310) 533-8877 and we can look it up for you.
Switching Your HMO Primary Care Doctor On HMO plans, your primary care doctor's name is printed on your ID card. You can select a new doctor inside your carrier's member portal under Find Care / Change Primary Doctor, or let our office know and we can update it for you.

5 Common Pitfalls When Comparing Plans

  1. Offering Only One Plan Option: Offering a single plan when pairing HMO, PPO, or HSA-compatible options can better fit different budgets and doctor preferences.
  2. Overlooking Local Doctor Group Rules: Not checking whether a specific carrier tier actually includes your doctors at Torrance Memorial (TMIPA), Providence Little Company of Mary, UCLA Health, or Optum South Bay.
  3. Renewing Without Comparing Other Carriers: Accepting annual rate increases without checking whether another carrier offers a better rate for the same doctors.
  4. Missing Tax-Free Health Stipends & HSAs: Overlooking Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), small-business tax credits, or tax-free monthly health allowances.
  5. Paying Full COBRA Rates Unnecessarily: Staying on expensive corporate COBRA when an individual Covered California or direct plan covers the exact same South Bay doctors for much less.
